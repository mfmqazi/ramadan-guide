Preface

اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ وَكَفٰى وَسَلَامٌ عَلٰى عِبَادِهِ الَّذِيْنَ اصْطَفٰى

Allah created human beings and He is their Sustainer. He not only sent down the means for man's physical sustenance but also sent down Divine guidance, guiding His creation in every aspect of life. The Creator of the universe also revealed that He created jinn and humans for His worship. For this purpose He sent prophets and through them, He taught His creation practical ways of worshipping Him, perfecting religion through the last Messenger - Muhammad ﷺ.

In acts of worship, Salah (prayer), Sawm (fasting), Hajj (pilgrimage) and Zakat (charity) were given an obligatory status. Whereas Salah is a daily act of worship, fasting is an annual one and the blessed month of Ramadan was chosen for it, a month which is superior to all other months. This book has been written to emphasize upon the virtues and importance of Ramadan so that through conscious effort we can fully avail the blessings and benefits of this month.

Farhat Hashmi

1st August, 2011

Shahru Ramadan: The Month of Ramadan

شَہۡرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِيۡۤ اُنۡزِلَ فِيۡهِ الۡقُرۡاٰنُ ہُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ وَ بَيِّنٰتٍ مِّنَ الۡہُدٰی وَ الۡفُرۡقَانِۚ فَمَنۡ شَہِدَ مِنۡكُمُ الشَّہۡرَ فَلۡيَصُمۡہُؕ وَ مَنۡ كَانَ مَرِيۡضًا اَوۡ عَلٰی سَفَرٍ فَعِدَّةٌ مِّنۡ اَيَّامٍ اُخَرَؕ يُرِيۡدُ اللّٰہُ بِكُمُ الۡيُسۡرَ وَ لَا يُرِيۡدُ بِكُمُ الۡعُسۡرَ۫ وَ لِتُكۡمِلُوا الۡعِدَّةَ وَ لِتُكَبِّرُوا اللّٰہَ عَلٰی مَا ہَدٰىكُمۡ وَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُوۡنَ

"The month of Ramadan [is that] in which was revealed the Qur'an, a guidance for the people and clear proofs of guidance and criterion. So whoever sights [the new moon of] the month, let him fast it; and whoever is ill or on a journey - then an equal number of other days. Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship and [wants] for you to complete the period and to glorify Allah for that [to] which He has guided you; and perhaps you will be grateful." [Al-Baqarah: 185]

Marhaban Ramadan: Welcome!

With the sighting of the moon of Sha'ban, one can feel the month of Ramadan approaching. Hearts begin to fill with joy and anticipation. Indeed an honourable guest is about to arrive, bringing much mercy, blessings and privilege.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "When the month of Ramadan arrives, a caller cries out: 'O seeker of good, proceed; O seeker of evil, desist'." [Sunan al-Tirmidhi: 682]

Things to Do Before Ramadan:

  • Fast in Sha'ban: Fast in the first half of Sha'ban (to make up missed fasts etc).
  • Seek Knowledge: Educate yourself about the rulings and etiquette of fasting.
  • Systematic Preparation: Prepare your family, clean your house, and organize your tasks beforehand.
  • Complete Worldly Tasks: Finish shopping or major chores so worship is not interrupted.
  • Set Goals: Allocate time for Qur'an, dhikr, supplication, and charity.

Istiqbalu Ramadan: Welcoming Invocations

Determine the beginning of Ramadan by sighting the new moon. Welcome the month with joy and make the following invocations:

اَللّٰهُمَّ اَهِلَّهٗ عَلَيْنَا بِالْيُمْنِ وَالْاِيْمَانِ وَالسَّلَامَةِ وَالْاِسْلَامِ رَبِّی وَرَبُّكَ اللّٰهُ

"O Allah, let this moon (month) pass over us with blessings, Iman, safety, and in the state of Islam. (O Moon!) My Lord and your Lord is Allah." [Sunan at-Tirmidhi: 3451]

The Month of Mercy & Blessing

Ramadan is the month in which Allah showers His special mercy. The devils are chained and the gates of mercy are opened.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "When there comes the month of Ramadan, the gates of mercy are opened." [Sahih Muslim: 2496]

Dua for Morning & Evening:

يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّوْمُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ اَسْتَغِيْثُ اَصْلِحْ لِيْ شَاْنِيْ كُلَّهٗ وَلَا تَكِلْنِيْ اِلٰی نَفْسِيْ طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ

"O Ever Living One, O Eternal One, by Your mercy I call on You to set right all my affairs. Do not hand me over to my soul even for the blinking of an eye." [Sunan al-Kubra: 10404]

Shahr al-Siyam: The Month of Fasting

Fasting (Siyam) is a special act of worship made mandatory in Sha'ban, 2 A.H. It is one of the five pillars of Islam.

يٰۤاَيُّہَا الَّذِيۡنَ اٰمَنُوۡا كُتِبَ عَلَيۡكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَی الَّذِيۡنَ مِنۡ قَبۡلِكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَتَّقُوۡنَ

"O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you that you may become righteous." [Al-Baqarah: 183]

Things to Do While Fasting:

  • Maintain sincere intention (Niyyah).
  • Guard your tongue and eyes from sin.
  • Avoid useless talk and arguments.
  • Focus on character development.

Al-Suhur wa al-Iftar

Fasting trains us in obedience. Refraining from food until a certain time and eating at Suhur even if one doesn't feel like it are acts of worship.

The Blessing of Suhur

"Take the Suhur meal, for there is in it (much) blessing." [Sahih al-Bukhari: 1923]

The Joy of Iftar

"The people will continue to be upon good as long as they hasten the breaking of the fast." [Sahih al-Bukhari: 1957]

Dua for Breaking the Fast (Iftar):

ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ

"The thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills." [Sunan Abu Dawud: 2357]

The Month of Forgiveness

A human being is prone to err. Ramadan is the perfect time to relieve ourselves of the burden of sins and seek cleanliness from Allah.

"Whoever fasts Ramadan out of faith and in the hope of reward, his previous sins will be forgiven." [Sahih al-Bukhari: 38]

The Month of Piety (Taqwa)

The core purpose of fasting is to attain Taqwa (God-consciousness). It is a shield against the fire and against sins in this world.

Fasting helps us distinguish between our true needs and our mere desires, strengthening our willpower to obey Allah.

The Month of Patience (Sabr)

Ramadan is known as the month of patience. Fasting requires patience in fulfilling Allah's commands and refraining from His prohibitions.

"Fasting is half of patience." [Sunan at-Tirmidhi: 3519]

The Month of Qur'an

Ramadan and the Qur'an are deeply interlinked. The Qur'an was revealed in this month, and Jibril (A.S) used to review the Qur'an with the Prophet ﷺ every Ramadan.

  • Listen to the Qur'an with attention.
  • Recite it with tajwid.
  • Understand the meanings and reflect.
  • Apply the teachings in your life.

The Month of Supplication (Du'a)

Ramadan is a time for heartfelt communication with Allah. The prayers of the fasting person are answered.

Dua for Forgiveness:

رَبَّنَا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَكَفِّرْ عَنَّا سَيِّئَاتِنَا وَتَوَفَّنَا مَعَ الْأَبْرَارِ

"Our Lord, so forgive us our sins and remove from us our evil deeds and cause us to die with the righteous." [Al-Imran: 193]

The Month of Remembrance (Dhikr)

Keep your tongue moist with the remembrance of Allah. Mentioning Allah brings peace to the heart.

سُبْحَانَ اللّٰهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ، وَلَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللّٰهُ، وَاللّٰهُ اَکْبَرُ

Glory be to Allah, Praise be to Allah, There is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest.

The Month of Charity (Sadaqah)

The Prophet ﷺ was the most generous of people, and he was even more generous during Ramadan.

  • Feed the poor and the fasting people.
  • Help those in need without being asked.
  • Donate to causes that spread the message of Islam.

The Month of Standing in Worship (Qiyam)

Standing in prayer at night (Taraweeh) is a great blessing of this month.

"Whoever stands (in prayer) during Ramadan with faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven." [Sahih al-Bukhari: 37]

The Last Ten Days

The Prophet ﷺ used to exert himself in worship during the last ten days more than at any other time.

  • Seek Laylatul Qadr in the odd nights.
  • Perform I'tikaf if possible.
  • Wake up your family for worship.
  • Tighten your belt for extra effort.

Laylatul-Qadr: The Night of Decree

A night better than a thousand months. It is the pinnacle of the Ramadan experience.

The Special Dua for this Night:

اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّی

"O Allah, You are Most Forgiving, and You love forgiveness, so forgive me." [Sunan at-Tirmidhi: 3513]

Farewell Ramadan & Self-Analysis

As the month departs, we should reflect on our state. Have we changed for the better? We should leave the month with gratitude and a strong resolve to continue our good deeds.

Eid al-Fitr & Takbirat

A day of celebration and gratitude to Allah for the success of completing the fast.

Takbirat of Eid:

اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ اَکْبَرُ اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ وَلِلّٰهِ الْحَمْدُ

"Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. There is no deity but Allah. Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, and all praises are for Allah."
